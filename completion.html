Tips to get there faster

📱 Scan everything early

Get into the habit of scanning cars, boats and planes with the WAiNK app as you drive. 200 vehicles is a lot to hunt down later.

🔭 Study, don't hunt

Looking at an animal through binoculars is enough to "study" it. You only need to hunt for Bret's quests.

🧘 Meditate twice

Meditate at each spot with both Jason and Lucia. Each character counts separately.

✅ Do optional missions as they appear

They count towards the 100 optional tasks, and you'll pass that number naturally.

🏁 Leave challenges for last

Many complete on their own as you play. You can skip the 10 most annoying of the 80.

🏦 Hardest parts

The 8 bank vaults (they need the endgame bank mission) and Bret's full hunting questline take the longest.
