Things worth knowing in your first hours in Leonida, and how to keep money coming in, because GTA 6 cares about cash more than any GTA before it.

Beginner tips

1 · Save manually, often

You can't replay missions. Keep a separate save slot for each chapter start, and save before heists and missable side jobs.

2 · Cash on hand vs the bank

GTA 6 separates the cash in your pocket from money in the bank. Cash can be lost when you die, so stash it in a safe house or bank it. Use ATMs around Leonida to withdraw.

3 · Change clothes to lose heat

Your Criminal Profile tracks how recognisable you are. Changing outfits after a crime helps you avoid being spotted.

4 · Watch out for tracked cars

Some vehicles carry trackers that lead police to you. Get a reliable car of your own early. Leonida is much bigger than Los Santos.

5 · Long days and nights

The day–night cycle is much longer than in past games. Sleep at Jason's house to skip ahead and pick the time of day.

6 · Do missables first

Before pushing the main story forward, check the map for hourglass icons. Those missions disappear. See the list →

Money guide

Some story missions won't start until you have enough money. For example, Chapter 4 asks you to pay a lawyer's fee after Lawyer Up. Keep a cash buffer.
