Why do sites list different counts? We use the 91-mission main story list reported by PC Gamer and Dexerto. Some guides count 93–97 because they add heist setups and optional story jobs. Some Chapter 3 and 4 missions can also show up in a different order depending on your choices.

Missable missions ⏳

These optional jobs are time-limited. They show an hourglass icon on the map and disappear for good once the story moves past them. Missing one can cost you the Strangers & Freaks trophy.

Mission tips that apply everywhere

💾 No replays

Missions can't be replayed and there are no gold medals. Make a manual save before big missions and at each chapter boundary.

💵 Cash gates

Some story steps need money. In Chapter 4 you pay a lawyer's fee after Lawyer Up, and there's another cash requirement in Chapter 5. Don't spend everything.

🔁 Character switching

Some missions are Jason-only or Lucia-only. Heist setups, especially for VCIA Takedown, make you switch between them several times.

🔀 Order can change

In Chapters 3 and 4 some missions run in parallel, so your order may not match this list exactly. Treat it as a guide.
