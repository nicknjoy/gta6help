About us

GTA6Help.in is an independent, fan-made help guide for Grand Theft Auto VI, built for players who get stuck on a mission, want to find every side quest, or are working towards 100%.

What we do

We keep spoiler-safe mission walkthroughs, side-mission lists, cheat codes, a 100% checklist and beginner tips in one simple, fast site. The site is updated daily as more of the game gets documented.

How we write our guides

Every guide is written in our own words and checked against several reputable sources before it goes up. Where a detail hasn't been confirmed yet, like a cheat code, we say so rather than guess.
