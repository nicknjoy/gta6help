What is GTA 6 about?

Grand Theft Auto VI is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida, and centres on Vice City. You play as two criminal partners, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. The story runs across five chapters, and you switch between the two leads as it goes.

It came out on 19 November 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version hasn't been announced yet.
