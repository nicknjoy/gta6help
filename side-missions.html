Strangers & Freaks

These are GTA 6's side-quest chains. For the Strangers & Freaks trophy, the questlines of Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count. Several other chains have trophies of their own:

Bret's Hunting Decks

Jason only. Hunting quests. Finding all 15 target decks is also needed for 100%. Trophy: Fair Game.

Hometown Heroes

Billy's questline. Trophy: The Lone Rider.

Puff Puff Pass

Andy's questline. Trophy: Road to Rehabilitation.

Randy's Wish List

Vehicle requests for Randy. Trophy: Supply & Demand.

Spin On This

Tyrone's questline. Trophy: Spin On This!

Yago & Kyle

Racing (Yago) and farm/chemicals (Kyle). Both count towards Strangers & Freaks.
