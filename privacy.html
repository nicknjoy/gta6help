Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information GTA6Help.in ("we", "us") collects when you visit https://gta6help.in, and how it is used.

Information we collect

We don't ask you to create an account and we don't collect your name, email or other personal details unless you choose to email us. If you do, we use your message only to reply to you.

Mission tracker

When you tick off missions, your progress is saved in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and we can't see it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Hosting and logs

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like most web hosts, Vercel may process basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and keep the service secure.

Visitor statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which guides are most useful. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally; it only records anonymous, aggregated data such as the page visited, the referring site, country and device type.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps the site free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors from regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland) will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to other websites, such as YouTube. We're not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

GTA 6 is rated for adults (18+). This site is not directed at children and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
