Editions & price (US)

Standard Edition · $79.99

The base game, digital or as a boxed code. You can upgrade to Ultimate later for the $20 difference.

Ultimate Edition · $99.99

Adds in-game extras tied to Jason and Lucia's story: vehicles, weapons, outfits and perks. No physical extras.

Indian prices vary by store. Check the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store for current ₹ pricing. Launch bonuses: the Vintage Vice City Pack for purchases before 20 November, and one free month of GTA+ with digital pre-orders, which renews as a paid subscription unless you cancel.

Characters

Jason Duval

One of the two leads. You play mostly as Jason through Chapter 1, with Lucia joining in later missions.

Lucia Caminos

The other lead. She opens the game in the Prologue and carries most of Chapter 2.

FAQ

How many missions are in GTA 6?

91 main story missions across five chapters (22, 11, 26, 25 and 7). Counts that include heist setups and optional jobs run higher. Full list →

Can I replay missions?

No. There's no mission replay, so keep manual saves.

Is GTA 6 on PC or PS4?

No. It's PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only for now. No PC version has been announced, and it isn't coming to PS4 or Xbox One.

How big is the download?

Rockstar hasn't published an official file size. Numbers you see online are unofficial.

Do cheats disable trophies?

Yes. While a cheat is active, trophies/achievements and saving are both turned off. All cheat codes →

Why was GTA 6 delayed?

It was first aimed at autumn 2025, moved to 26 May 2026, then to 19 November 2026. Rockstar said it needed the extra time for polish.
